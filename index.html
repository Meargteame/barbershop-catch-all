Common Questions

Do I have to switch away from my current booking software? +

No. We do not replace your booking system. We build custom automation that wires directly into Fresha, Boulevard, Square, Vagaro, Mindbody, Mangomint, or Acuity. Your team keeps their exact calendar and daily workflow.

Will it sound like a generic, robotic chatbot to my clients? +

Not at all. We build and train the system specifically around your shop’s tone, terminology, service menu, pricing, and stylist names. Replies read like an experienced front-desk coordinator, not a bot.

What happens if a customer asks a complex or unusual question? +

If an inquiry requires human judgment (like a custom service or VIP request), the automation instantly alerts your team with the client’s details so you or a staff member can step in seamlessly.

Never. We work strictly month-to-month. If the system we build isn’t recovering lost bookings and paying for itself every single month, you can cancel at any time with zero penalty.

How quickly can our shop go live? +

Setup typically takes 24 to 48 hours. After our 15-minute walkthrough, we build and test the entire integration for you behind the scenes — zero technical work is required on your end.
